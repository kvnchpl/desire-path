About

What is DESIRE PATH?
It is an experimental cartographic artwork about the different ways encounters become near. Rather than beginning with an overview, it reveals a landscape one encounter at a time.
How do I move through the landscape?
Follow an arrowed path from the current encounter to one nearby. On a touch device, tap once to reveal an encounter's title and again to follow its path.
What makes two encounters near?
The selected dimensions do. Place considers geographic distance; time, feeling, and knowing offer other forms of closeness. Every selected dimension contributes equally.
Why do the available paths change?
Each combination of dimensions produces a different set of nearby encounters. Selecting or clearing a dimension redraws the possible paths without moving the encounters themselves.
Can I turn off every dimension?
No. At least one way of exploring must remain active so the landscape can determine what is near.
What do the colors and arrows mean?
Color quietly reflects the active dimensions and their mixtures. Arrowheads point toward encounters you can visit from your current position.
What does Retrace do?
Retrace returns to the encounter you visited immediately before this one. You can use it repeatedly to follow your journey backward.
What can I find under Options?
You can reveal every encounter's position or view the current encounter's place, time, feeling, and knowing. Advanced options can also show encounter IDs and every potential path; those additional paths are references, not navigable routes.
Why can't I see the whole landscape at once?
The partial view is intentional. The work favors exploration over search and local knowledge over overview, allowing the landscape to accumulate as you move through it.
